## 2. Case-specific decisions and non-negotiable corrections

### Case 01 — Sulli Deals & Bulli Bai
- Separate the July 2021 Sulli Deals incident from the January 2022 Bulli Bai incident and distinguish perpetrators.
- Do not claim that investigative delay in the first incident caused the second.
- Describe GitHub cross-border data process with qualified wording. Contemporary reporting attributed the initial direct-request / formal legal-process timeline to police sources; GitHub's transparency report supports its general policy for foreign law-enforcement requests, not the precise case-specific response date.
- Do not say GitHub simply refused cooperation unless supported by case-specific correspondence.
- Include the Supreme Court's 1 December 2025 disposal of the connected writ petitions only for what the order actually decided.
- Obtain primary copies of relevant 2026 magistrate orders before describing their reasoning in detail.
- Preserve pseudonymization and trial-status safeguards.

### Case 02 — Jaiprakash Kulkarni
- Use Bombay High Court W.P. 1150/2023, 13 June 2024.
- Correct neutral citation to `2024:BHC-OS:8627-DB`.
- Distinguish ₹76,90,017 ordered refunded plus 6% annual interest from the Cyber Cell transaction figure of ₹76,90,444.70.
- Describe notification evidence accurately: beneficiary-addition alerts were not delivered; transaction/OTP messages were sent to the registered mobile on 2 October; relevant bank emails were not received at the registered email.
- Do not simplify the finding to “OTP never arrived”.
- Explain the Ombudsman's failure to inquire into actual notification delivery.

### Case 03 — Cyberabad instant micro-lending
- Include Telangana High Court W.P. (PIL) 13/2021 and its 9 November 2021 disposal; accurately attribute police activity reported in the DGP affidavit.
- Include *Wecash Technology Pvt. Ltd. v. State of Telangana*, Criminal Petition 3588/2021, 5 July 2021, and its six police-case references. These references do not prove six deaths.
- Do not publish an unverified aggregate death count or assert legal causation of individual suicides.
- Include ED's 23 August 2024 prosecution-complaint announcement and 22 August 2024 cognizance, attributing allegations to the ED.
- Include *Smartcoin* (6 November 2024) and *Krazybee* (11 March 2025) with petitioner-specific scope. Neither judgment exonerates the entire digital lending sector.
- Include the Appellate Tribunal order of 5 January 2026, which dismissed appeals and upheld the challenged provisional attachment decisions in respect of the NBFC-fintech arrangements before it. Do not call attachment findings criminal convictions.
- Update current regulatory explanation to RBI Digital Lending Directions, 2025 (issued 8 May 2025); distinguish historical rules from current rules.
- Do not infer police negligence merely from regulatory gaps or the existence of multiple investigations.
- Obtain the official judgments, ED prosecution complaint and current case-status records before final publication.

### Case 04 — Harshada Bindu Dholepatil
- Use TDSAT Cyber Appeal 7/2019, judgment dated 25 September 2024, not a High Court judgment.
- Explain identity-document mismatch in the duplicate SIM process.
- Describe SIM-swap-enabled financial fraud, but do not claim the tribunal established every technical detail of OTP interception.
- Separate telecom compensation liability from criminal investigation and bank liability.
- Correct compensation to ₹3,00,000 plus 12% compound interest, as specified in the judgment.

### Case 05 — AePS biometric cloned-deed exploits
- Use Hyderabad Police press note (22 November 2023) and Haryana Government memorandum as primary sources.
- Attribute the reported approximate figures (2,500 land-registration files, 1,000 cloned fingerprints, 2,500 transactions, ₹10 lakh loss, six apprehended, ₹5 lakh returned at that time) to police, not final judicial findings.
- Distinguish public exposure of registry documents / fingerprints and spoofing from a breach of UIDAI's central Aadhaar database. No central database breach is established by the cited material.
- Include MHA parliamentary response on AePS fraud reporting and security controls.
- Remove unsupported claim that NPCI introduced universal liveness detection from 1 January 2024.
- Explain RBI AePS Touchpoint Operator Due Diligence Directions, 2025, issued 27 June 2025 and effective 1 January 2026.

### Case 06 — AIIMS New Delhi ransomware
- Use the official MoHFW Lok Sabha UQ 1837 (16 December 2022) and MeitY Lok Sabha UQ 2310 (21 December 2022).
- Explain five affected physical NIC e-Hospital servers; backup server unaffected; restoration to new servers; roughly two weeks to restore functions; manual operations during disruption.
- Attribute the 1.3 TB figure to the official record as encrypted data. Encryption does not establish exfiltration.
- Remove ₹200 crore ransom demand as a fact. Government stated no specific ransom amount was demanded.
- Attribute improper network segmentation as a contributing factor as stated by MeitY.
- Do not attribute the incident to China or another state actor without reliable evidence.

### Case 07 — Pegasus technical committee inquiry
- Use the Supreme Court judgment of 27 October 2021 and proceedings of 25 August 2022 as primary references.
- Distinguish a leaked list of potential targets from confirmed infection.
- Report 29 devices examined and malware-related findings on five devices only with the qualification that Pegasus infection could not be conclusively established.
- Attribute non-cooperation to the committee report / Court's public observations; do not infer Government deployment.
- Full technical reports were submitted in sealed covers; do not invent their contents.
- Latest Supreme Court docket / final disposal status was not conclusively verified in the research reviewed. Retain a publication status caveat until a current docket or final order is obtained.

### Case 08 — Ravinder Singh @ Kaku
- Use official Supreme Court judgment, Criminal Appeal 1307/2019 and connected appeals, 4 May 2022.
- Explain acquittal as the result of multiple evidentiary weaknesses, not only the missing Section 65B certificate.
- Include the original-device exception under the *Arjun Panditrao* interpretation.
- Explain historical IEA Sections 65A/65B separately from current BSA Sections 62/63.
- Include the BSA Schedule certificate and Section 170(2) transitional rule.
- Distinguish admissibility from evidentiary weight.

### Case 09 — CFCFRMS / 1930 restitution
- Use February 2024 Parliamentary Standing Committee on Communications & IT 54th Report for historical figures (as of 31 October 2023).
- Distinguish amount reported lost, lien-marked, held/saved and actually returned.
- Include official Money Restoration Portal, January 2026 SOP existence, April 2026 MRM/GRM and later MHA figures with exact reporting dates and definitions.
- Explain CrPC Sections 451/457 and BNSS Sections 497/503; explain BNSS Section 107 separately.
- Do not imply automatic entitlement to refunds or a universal Magistrate-order requirement.
- Exclude the unverified 6 August 2024 Lok Sabha UQ 2577 / ₹11,000 crore attribution.
- Note that the complete January 2026 SOP text was not located in the research reviewed.

### Case 10 — Kudankulam administrative network malware
- Use official DAE, NPCIL, CERT-In and parliamentary records.
- Correct the parliamentary reference: do not cite Lok Sabha UQ 1297 as the definitive record. Include Lok Sabha UQ 659 (20 November 2019), Rajya Sabha Q 481 and 483 (21 November 2019), DAE reply (27 November 2019) and Rajya Sabha Starred Q 109 (28 November 2019), as applicable.
- Distinguish the infected Internet-connected administrative PC/network from isolated plant control and instrumentation systems.
- State that Government/NPCIL reported no impact on critical plant systems.
- Explain the 29–30 October 2019 public communication sequence without claiming the reactor was hacked.
- Date and scope the 2019 and 2022 security measures accurately.
- Do not present Dtrack, state attribution, exfiltration or operational disruption as official findings unless the specific primary source establishes them.
