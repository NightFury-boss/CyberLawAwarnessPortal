## Evidence rule

Primary judgments, tribunal orders, parliamentary answers, official government releases and regulator directions take precedence. Secondary reporting may help locate a document but must not be presented as a primary finding. Distinguish:

- established judicial or official findings;
- allegations made by a party or investigating agency;
- submissions recorded by a court or tribunal;
- reporting not independently verified against the underlying order;
- editorial inference and open research questions.

If a primary source cannot be retrieved, retain the caveat and mark the item as a publication verification task. Do not fill gaps from general knowledge.
